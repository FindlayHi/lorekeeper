# Borderlands 3 Vault Card challenges: where to farm each daily and weekly challenge

## How Vault Card daily and weekly challenges work

Vault Cards came with the Director's Cut add-on. There are three: Vault Card 1 (Fallen Heroes), Vault Card 2 (Welcome to Pandora) and Vault Card 3 (Bunker Masters). Each card shows three daily challenges and one weekly challenge, drawn from a large rotating pool, and only the card you have activated makes progress, so switch to the card you want before you play. A daily challenge pays 50 Eridium and a weekly challenge 1,100 Eridium. Daily challenges reset at 9:00 AM Pacific every day, weekly challenges at 9:00 AM Pacific every Thursday. Every card level gives a Vault Card chest, which mostly holds that card's keys and cosmetics, sometimes Eridium or a Diamond Key; a card's keys only unlock that card's gear.

## Vault Card challenge: kills with an element, a manufacturer or a weapon type

Challenges such as "kill enemies with shock damage", "kill enemies with Jakobs weapons" or "kill enemies with a shotgun" count anywhere, so go where enemies are packed close and respawn. Lectra City on Promethea is the usual suggestion, and the Trials on Sanctuary 3 and the Circles of Slaughter (Slaughter Shaft, Cistern of Slaughter, Slaughterstar 3000) work too, as does the Claptrap Nest in the Moxxi's Heist of the Handsome Jackpot add-on: equip the right element, brand or gun type and run waves.

## Vault Card challenge: vehicle kills

For "kill enemies with a vehicle", take a vehicle round The Droughts on Pandora and run over or shoot the enemies there.

## Vault Card challenge: kill Badasses

Badasses turn up in every wave-based arena. Any Circle of Slaughter (Slaughter Shaft on Pandora, Cistern of Slaughter on Promethea, Slaughterstar 3000 on Sanctuary 3) gives a steady supply of them.

## Vault Card challenge: kill Psychos, Enforcers, Heavies, Nogs and Tinks

- Psychos and Enforcers: the Slaughter Shaft on Pandora (its waves are Children of the Vault). It also counts for "kill COV" and "kill enemies on Pandora".
- Heavies: Athenas, or Slaughterstar 3000.
- Nogs: Slaughterstar 3000 on Sanctuary 3.
- Tinks: The Anvil on Eden-6.

## Vault Card challenge: kill Skags, Rakk, Spiderants, Varkids, Goliaths and Hags

- Skags and Rakk: The Droughts on Pandora.
- Spiderants: Devil's Razor on Pandora, around where Antalope spawns.
- Varkids: Konrad's Hold on Pandora, near Manvark, or the Cistern of Slaughter on Promethea.
- Goliaths: The Splinterlands on Pandora, where Road Dog spawns, or Jakobs Estate on Eden-6.
- Hags: Konrad's Hold on Pandora.

## Vault Card challenge: kill Saurians, Jabbers, Ratch, Service Bots and Maliwan Mechs

- Saurians: the Cistern of Slaughter on Promethea, or the Voracious Canopy on Eden-6.
- Jabbers: the Cistern of Slaughter, the Trial of Instinct, or Ambermire on Eden-6 near Red Jabber.
- Ratch: Meridian Outskirts (the first area) or Meridian Metroplex on Promethea, or the Maliwan Takedown at Midnight's Cairn.
- Service Bots: Lectra City or Meridian Metroplex on Promethea.
- Maliwan Mechs: farm GenIVIV in the Voracious Canopy on Eden-6. For "kill Maliwan", Athenas is full of them.

## Vault Card challenge: kill Guardians, Nekrobugs and Overspheres

- Guardians: the Guardian Takedown on Minos Prime, or the end of Neon Arterial on Promethea, reached by going back through from the Forgotten Basilica.
- Nekrobugs: Nekrotafeyo, in Tazendeer Ruins or Desolation's Edge.
- Overspheres: the Trial of Discipline (Precipice Anchor, Sanctuary 3).

## Vault Card challenge: kill raid bosses

The weekly "kill raid bosses" challenge counts the Invincibles and the Takedown bosses:
- Hemovorous the Invincible: Darkthirst Dominion on Pandora (the door costs Eridium). Killing only Hemovorous and leaving Vermivorous alone is a fast way to stack raid boss kills.
- Wotan the Invincible: the Maliwan Takedown at Midnight's Cairn.
- Scourge the Invincible Martyr and Anathema the Relentless: the Guardian Takedown on Minos Prime.

## Vault Card challenge: kill a named enemy

A daily can ask for one named enemy. Where the common ones are:
- Baron Noggin: Meridian Metroplex on Promethea.
- Billy, the Anointed: Jakobs Estate on Eden-6, in the theatre.
- Borman Nates: Meridian Outskirts on Promethea.
- Captain Traunt: Athenas, at the far end of the map past the long bridge.
- Troy Calypso: The Great Vault on Pandora. Tyreen Calypso: Destroyer's Rift on Pandora.
- The Valkyries: the Maliwan Takedown at Midnight's Cairn.
For a challenge to kill many bosses, kill one quick boss over and over: Shiv (Covenant Pass on Pandora) and Gigamind (Meridian Metroplex on Promethea) are the fastest.

## Vault Card challenge: complete a Trial or a Circle of Slaughter

- Slaughter Shaft: off Konrad's Hold on Pandora. Cistern of Slaughter: off Meridian Metroplex on Promethea. Slaughterstar 3000: Sanctuary 3.
- Trials, all reached from Sanctuary 3: Trial of Cunning (Ghostlight Beacon), Trial of Discipline (Precipice Anchor), Trial of Fervor (The Skydrowned Pulpit), Trial of Instinct (Wayward Tether), Trial of Supremacy (The Hall Obsidian), Trial of Survival (Gradient of Dawn).

## Vault Card challenge: kills on a planet or against a faction

- Kill COV: the Slaughter Shaft on Pandora.
- Kill Maliwan: Athenas.
- Kill Guardians: the Guardian Takedown on Minos Prime.
- Kill enemies on Pandora: the Slaughter Shaft, or The Droughts if you don't have it.
- Kill enemies on Promethea: the Cistern of Slaughter.
- Kill enemies on Eden-6: The Anvil, which is one big farm of Badasses and Anointed; Jakobs Estate and Floodmoor Basin's COV outposts are busy too.
- Kill enemies on Nekrotafeyo: Tazendeer Ruins.

## Vault Card challenge: open red chests

Stormblind Complex is the suggested place to open red chests.

## Levelling a Vault Card fast (XP)

A Vault Card levels from the same XP you earn anyway (kills, missions, challenges), but only the active card fills. The Slaughter Shaft is the steadiest XP: lots of enemies in a small space. Bosses like Graveward are quick kills for a strong character. Mayhem level does not change the XP you get, so it can be off. XP boosts stack: the Borderlands Science machine's 25 % tokens and a Moxxi's Endowment artifact.
